# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Orbit a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, welcome different viewpoints, give
actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention,
threats, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security contact. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit
participation when needed to protect the community.
